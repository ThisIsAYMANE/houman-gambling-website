🎮 Slotegrator Validation Guide

⚠️ Important: Follow these steps exactly as described by Slotegrator support.
  1. 1
    Launch Game with /games/init
    Open the /games/init endpoint in your browser to create a game session.
  2. 2
    Wait for Game to Load
    Do NOT interact with the game. Just wait for it to fully load and stay on the game page.
  3. 3
    Send POST /self-validate
    Send the validation request. This will test your callback handlers.
  4. 4
    Check Results
    Look for the keyword "failed" in the log, or check for status: 1 and success: true
